Our story

A small Austin crew that treats your home like our own

BrightNest started in 2016 when our founder, Carla, was cleaning houses on weekends and kept hearing the same two complaints: prices that changed at the door, and a different stranger every visit.

So we built the opposite. Prices are printed on this page and stay put. Your crew is the same two people each time, employed by us — not gig workers — and they learn how you like your kitchen left.
